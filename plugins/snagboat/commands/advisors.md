Call the `mcp__plugin_snagboat_snagboat__snagboat_advisors` tool (with `days` set to $ARGUMENTS if that's a number, otherwise leave it out) and show each advisor on a few lines: its name and status, the measures that changed, and what it has to surface. Keep Snagboat's numbers exactly as given.
